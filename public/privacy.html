Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 19, 2026

Excalidrive is a free, open-source drawing app that saves your drawings either in your browser or in your own Google Drive. It has no backend server and no user accounts of its own.

What data the app accesses

  • Google Drive files created by Excalidrive. If you connect Google Drive, the app requests only the drive.file permission. This lets it create, read, update, and delete the folders and drawings it creates itself (inside a folder named "Excalidrive"). It cannot see any other files in your Drive.
  • Your Google name, email, and profile picture, used only to show which account is connected in the sidebar.

Where your data goes

  • Your drawings travel directly between your browser and Google Drive. They are never sent to, stored on, or seen by the developer or any server operated by Excalidrive.
  • If you choose "Skip for now", drawings are stored only in your browser's local storage on that device.
  • The temporary Google access token is kept in your browser's session storage and is cleared when you close the tab or disconnect.

What we don't do

  • No analytics, tracking, ads, or cookies set by Excalidrive.
  • We do not sell, share, or transfer your data to anyone.
  • We do not use your data to train AI models.

Third parties

The app loads Google's sign-in library from Google, and Excalidraw's fonts from a public CDN. Google Drive storage is governed by Google's Privacy Policy.

Google API Services

Excalidrive's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Removing your data

  • Delete drawings in the app, or delete the "Excalidrive" folder in Google Drive.
  • Revoke the app's access anytime at myaccount.google.com/permissions.
  • For browser-only drawings, clear this site's data in your browser settings.

Contact

Questions? Open an issue at github.com/toolsmyth/excalidrive/issues.
